### 💡 Core Drivers behind the Projections
1. **Compounding Growth Differential:** India maintains an estimated **6.5% to 7% real compound annual growth rate**. In contrast, most Latin American and African nations listed are constrained to historic levels of **1% to 2.5%**.
2. **Demographic Advantages:** Rapidly aging workforces in places like **Thailand** and portions of Eastern Europe act as structural caps on long-term GDP acceleration.
3. **Economic Diversification:** Resource-dependent economies (e.g., **Algeria, Iraq, Gabon**) face growth thresholds unless they scale complex services or manufacturing hubs.
